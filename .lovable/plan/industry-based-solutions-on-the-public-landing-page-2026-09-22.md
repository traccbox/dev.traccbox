# Industry-Based Solutions on the public landing page

## What will change
- Add a new “Industry Solutions” section after the current feature overview.
- Present the three supplied solution groups as an interactive, mobile-friendly selector:
  - Construction & Security
  - Logistics, Distribution, Delivery & Supply Chain
  - Fintech, FMCG & Telecommunications
- Show each industry’s business outcome, strongest capabilities, and a clear link to start a trial or contact TraccBox.
- Add “Industries” to the desktop and mobile navigation, plus the footer quick links.
- Use the supplied TraccBox logo as the section’s branded visual signature.
- Create one polished, realistic panoramic image showing the three field-work contexts, without replacing any existing landing-page visuals.

## Interaction and layout
- Desktop: concise industry selector beside a large visual and detailed capability panel.
- Mobile: stacked selector and content with comfortable tap targets and no hidden information.
- Switching industries updates the headline, description, feature list, and highlighted visual label without leaving the page.
- Keep the current blue design language, rounded cards, typography, animations, and conversion flow.
